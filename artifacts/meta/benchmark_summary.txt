Limitations:
  [1] Annual data (≤29 obs/node) limits generalisation; results may differ for higher-frequency domains (daily, weekly).
  [2] FedAvg-AR1 is the federation baseline; Flower/SCAFFOLD or async-FedAvg would be stronger comparisons for future work.
  [3] Scarcity's prediction uses lag-1 (last observed value); a dedicated prediction head using high-confidence hypotheses would improve MAE.
  [4] Synthetic data (--dry-run) uses a random-walk generator; real WB API data may show different cross-country correlation structure.
  [5] Federation simulation shares raw data rows as a proxy for parameter sharing; full ws_transport.py evaluation is future work.
  [6] No differential privacy budget is measured or enforced in this benchmark.
  [7] World Bank indicators have known measurement gaps, especially for Uganda before 2000; missing values are dropped silently.
